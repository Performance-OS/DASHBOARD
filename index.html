Race Setup

Easy = conversational, most of your weekly volume. Long run = slightly easier than goal pace, for endurance. Tempo = comfortably hard, sustained effort. Interval = short, fast reps for speed & VO2max.

HR ranges are calibrated from your actual recent runs, not the generic Strava zone chart. "Now" reflects what's realistic today; "Goal" is what to work toward as fitness builds. Lead with pace/conversational effort; only back off if HR runs far outside the "Now" range.

Training Block Marathon

Your zones, plan table, and Home tab all follow whichever block is active. Only one is active at a time — start a new one when you're ready to switch distances, and the current block gets archived, not deleted.

This archives your current block — plan, dates, completions, nothing lost — and generates a full progressive plan for the new goal: build phase, periodic deload weeks, taper, and race day, sized to fit however many weeks are left. Starting volume is calibrated from your recent Strava mileage where possible. It's a solid structural starting point, not a substitute for fine-tuning together in chat once you're actually in it.
